# Violet Horizons wallpaper catalogue

Open `index.html` in a browser. The catalogue contains 36 visually reviewed
wallpaper candidates organized into Natural, Fantasy & Mythic, and Space &
Sci-Fi categories.

The reference is Omarchy's installed Tokyo Night `0-winding-road.jpg`.
Candidate thumbnails are cached locally; full-resolution images remain at their
source pages. Starred candidates are saved in the browser's local storage and
can be exported as a text shortlist.

No full-resolution third-party artwork is installed here. Confirm an artist's
usage terms before downloading or reusing their work.
